Find what is missing from what I'm looking at, and build it. Work from the field's objective reality, not from what is already here — the aim is unknown-unknowns, the corners a reader with ordinary knowledge would not think to look in.

Read the curation rules first. Then, depending on where I am:

**A world with no map (or no datasets):** work out which fields make it up — the distinct fields of work a person training their eye here should know, spread across scale, era, region and kind, not just the famous corners. Propose the datasets I should start with (one-word topic, a description of its core idea, the minimum set of subtopics that together cover it), then draw the map: two axes that genuinely organise this world, 4–8 named regions, every dataset placed, and the further fields I'm missing marked as proposed missing fields with why each matters.

**A world with a map:** is the map still right, and what fields am I missing? Every region should hold the fields that actually make it up — favour the ones I wouldn't think of. Add them as proposed missing fields. Say if any datasets should be merged, split or renamed (two that are really one field; one that is really two; a misleading name) and stage that as create, move, delete-the-emptied-field. Change only what is wrong; a settled map is something I have learned my way around.

**A dataset:** map the field's real dimensions from your own knowledge — makers, movements, eras, regions, sub-genres, lineages — check that map against the web where you are unsure, then compare the dataset against it subtopic by subtopic and dimension by dimension. Where is it thin, lopsided, or recent-and-famous-heavy? Tell me the gaps plainly, biggest first. Then propose the defining items that fill them, in batches of about ten, every field filled, an imageQuery specific enough to find that exact item and year, years and attributions verified where you are not sure. Never repeat what is here. Favour the work that shaped the field over the work that is merely well known; if a gap is real but you cannot name a defining item for it with confidence, say so instead of padding.

Give me your reasoning in a few lines before you stage anything. If I gave a brief or a number below, that is the brief — follow it, and let the rules govern how you choose within it.
