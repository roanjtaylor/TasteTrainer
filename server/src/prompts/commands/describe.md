Fill in the thin and missing descriptions in this dataset.

Read every item in full. An item's description is the note on why it is great — what to look at and why it matters to the field. A good one is one or two sentences, concrete and specific to THIS item (what it did first, what it changed, what it does better than its peers), never generic praise. The defining fact is one sentence: the notable fact that gives the item its place in the field.

Propose new text only where the current text is missing, generic, wrong, or says nothing the name doesn't. Leave good notes alone, and never lengthen for its own sake. Check any fact you introduce.
