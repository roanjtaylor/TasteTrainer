Find what this dataset is missing. Work from the field's objective reality, not from what is already here.

1. Read the dataset in full and the curation rules.
2. Map the field's real dimensions from your own knowledge first — makers and brands, movements and schools, eras, regions, sub-genres, and the technical or formal lineages that matter. Then check that map against the web where you are unsure: the aim is unknown-unknowns, the corners a reader with ordinary knowledge would not think to look in.
3. Compare the dataset against that map, subtopic by subtopic AND dimension by dimension. Where is it thin, lopsided, or recent-and-famous-heavy? Which makers dominate that should have peers beside them? Which whole regions, eras or movements have nothing?
4. Tell me the gaps plainly, biggest first — a short paragraph or a tight list, no item-by-item recital.
5. Then propose the defining items that fill them, in batches, each with every field filled and a specific imageQuery. Favour the work that shaped the field over the work that is merely well known. Do not pad; if a gap is real but you cannot name a defining item for it with confidence, say so instead.
